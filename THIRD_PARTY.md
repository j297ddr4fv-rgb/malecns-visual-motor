# External models and datasets

- MaleCNS v1.0: CC BY 4.0; https://male-cns.janelia.org/download/ . Attribute the FlyEM Project Team (HHMI Janelia), Drosophila Connectomics Group (University of Cambridge/MRC LMB), and Google Research. See SOURCE_ASSETS.md for graph transformations, source files and checksums. The CSR graph and original connectome tables are not included in this public release.
- FlyVis 1.2.0 package code: MIT; https://github.com/TuragaLab/flyvis . The pretrained checkpoint is not redistributed; obtain it through the official downloader. Package code licensing does not establish checkpoint redistribution permission.
- FlyGym 2.1.0 / NeuroMechFly v2: Apache-2.0; https://github.com/NeLy-EPFL/flygym/tree/v2.1.0 . Download the runtime and body assets upstream. Neither is included here.
- The procedural stripe-panel scene in `replay/models/optomotor-panel/` is CC0-1.0 according to its source metadata.
- The model-derived T4/T5 target-direction mapping and the nine retinal input archives remain external pending confirmation of their redistribution terms. SOURCE_ASSETS.md records their identities. Public experimental output tables do not contain pretrained model weights.

Original project code is MIT. Original experimental data and figures are CC BY 4.0. Third-party rights are not relicensed by these grants. SOURCE_ASSETS.md records the provenance of the earlier local reproduction bundle; its references to included CSR/target/input assets describe that private bundle, not this public repository.
