# Neural initialization and gait endpoint rule

External Poisson drive was disabled in all behavioral and connection-structure experiments: the external event rate was 0 Hz for every neuron, and no cells received a Poisson schedule. Visual input entered as the mapped effective current described above; body mechanosensory input was not injected into the central network. At each run start, all membrane voltages were set to -52 mV, synaptic states and delayed-input buffers were zero, and refractory deadlines were zero. Neural initialization therefore used a point distribution, not randomized membrane voltages. FlyVis alone underwent 1 s of gray-scene preadaptation before recorded time; the central network and body were not advanced during that preadaptation.

For the applied steering value s, define a=|s| and select the left side when s>=0 and the right side when s<0. On that side, each leg's anterior extreme position (AEP) is displaced posteriorly by 0.05a mm and its posterior extreme position (PEP) is unchanged. On the opposite side, AEP is displaced anteriorly by 0.05a mm and PEP posteriorly by 0.05a mm. These offsets act along the thorax-local anterior-posterior axis. AEP and PEP phases are measured from each leg's native template using 512 phase samples. Between successive extrema the offset interpolates with h(u)=6u^5-15u^4+10u^3, where u is normalized phase progress on that segment, and is periodic over a cycle. The resulting Cartesian targets are converted to joint offsets by six position-only damped IK iterations, with 0.025-mm damping, a 0.04-rad per-iteration increment limit and a 0.35-rad final offset limit. Both engineered endpoint channels receive a; their DN-like implementation names do not imply biological muscle identification. Contact and hind-leg clearance corrections remain those of the fixed body controller. Thus P controls gait phase progression, while s changes the leg excursion geometry.

Implementation: `scripts/malecns_engine.py` (`_allocate_state`), `scripts/flygym_online_neural_worker_eye_parity_v3.py` (sparse sensory input), and `scripts/flygym_ap_endpoint_gesture_prototype.py` (`_endpoint_deltas_for_leg` and `endpoint_ap_delta`). The neuron-like names of the endpoint channels are engineering labels. Body contact feedback acts in the gait controller, while body pose determines retinal sampling; no central mechanosensory channel was supplied in these runs.
